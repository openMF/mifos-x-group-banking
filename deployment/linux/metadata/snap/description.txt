MifosSave digitizes the full life of a community savings group — from the first meeting to the final share-out — and works fully offline, syncing to a Mifos / Apache Fineract core banking backend the moment you reconnect.

Built for VSLAs, ROSCAs, ASCAs, SILC groups, SHGs, SACCOs, village banks, welfare and burial societies, and joint-liability groups, MifosSave replaces error-prone paper ledgers with transparent records every member can trust.

Features:

* Offline-first — run a whole meeting with no signal, then sync automatically.
* Create or join a group with an invite code; 9 group types with the right rules built in.
* Run meetings — attendance, group-linked and individual savings, fines, guided agenda.
* Real-time group corpus with excess-outflow blocking.
* Group loans — request, vote-approve, disburse, repay, with ceilings and repayment health.
* End-of-cycle share-out — pro-rata, rotation payout or auction.
* Personal dashboard, notifications, and English/Swahili/French/Hindi support.

MifosSave is open-source community banking software from the Mifos Initiative — keeping every shilling accounted for, online or off.
