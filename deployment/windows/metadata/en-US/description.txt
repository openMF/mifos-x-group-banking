MifosSave digitizes the full life of a community savings group — from the first meeting to the final share-out — and works fully offline, syncing to a Mifos / Apache Fineract core banking backend the moment you reconnect.

Built for VSLAs, ROSCAs, ASCAs, SILC groups, SHGs, SACCOs, village banks, welfare and burial societies, and joint-liability groups, MifosSave replaces error-prone paper ledgers with transparent records every member can trust.

WHY GROUPS SWITCH TO MIFOSSAVE
• Offline-first — run a whole meeting with no signal, then sync automatically.
• Meeting-centric — the app follows how your group really works: attendance, savings, loans, decisions, close-out.
• Transparent — every member sees group balances, loan status and share-out projections in real time.
• Built on proven open-source core banking (Mifos / Apache Fineract).
• Low-literacy friendly — icon-led screens, large touch targets, multi-language support.

CREATE OR JOIN A GROUP
• Sign up in minutes and create your first group, or join an existing one with an invite code.
• Choose your group type — VSLA, ASCA, ROSCA, SILC, SHG, SACCO, village bank, welfare society or JLG — each with the right rules built in.
• Invite members by link or code and assign roles: organizer, treasurer, chairperson, secretary or member.

RUN MEETINGS
• Schedule meetings, take attendance and work through a guided step-by-step agenda.
• Review the previous meeting before you begin.
• Record group-linked and individual savings as members contribute.
• Collect fines and track late attendance.

MANAGE SAVINGS & THE GROUP FUND
• Track the group corpus in real time, with excess-outflow blocking so you never disburse more than you hold.
• See opening and closing balances for every meeting.

LEND AND REPAY
• Members request loans; the group approves them by vote.
• Disburse, schedule repayments and track repayment health.
• Set loan ceilings and per-member limits.

SHARE OUT
• Calculate and distribute the end-of-cycle share-out — pro-rata for VSLAs, rotation payouts for ROSCAs, or auction/bid for chit groups.

STAY IN THE LOOP
• Personal dashboard for every member: your savings, your loans, your requests.
• Notifications for meetings, approvals and payouts.
• Available in English, Swahili, French and Hindi.

MifosSave is open-source community banking software from the Mifos Initiative. Whether you run one chama or supervise dozens of groups in the field, MifosSave keeps every shilling accounted for — online or off.
