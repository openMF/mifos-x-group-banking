Welcome to MifosSave 1.0 — offline-first banking for community savings groups.

Create or join a group with an invite code and choose from 9 group types (VSLA, ROSCA, SILC, SHG, SACCO and more). Run meetings, record savings, approve and repay group loans, track your group fund in real time, and complete end-of-cycle share-out — all offline, syncing to Mifos Fineract. Available in English, Swahili, French and Hindi.
