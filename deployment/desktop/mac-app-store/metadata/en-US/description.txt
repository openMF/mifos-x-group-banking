Your savings group, on your Mac — and it works even when the network doesn't.

MifosSave brings the full life of a community savings group to your desktop: meetings, savings, loans and share-out, all backed by proven open-source Mifos / Apache Fineract core banking and all working offline first.

Whether you run a VSLA, ROSCA, ASCA, SILC, SHG, SACCO, village bank, welfare society or joint-liability group, MifosSave replaces paper ledgers with records every member can see and trust.

GET STARTED IN MINUTES
• Sign up and create your first group, or join one with an invite code.
• Pick your group type — the right rules are built in.
• Invite members and assign roles: organizer, treasurer, chairperson, secretary or member.

RUN YOUR MEETINGS
• Take attendance, follow a guided agenda, and review the previous meeting first.
• Record group-linked and individual savings.
• Collect fines and track late attendance.

MANAGE THE GROUP FUND
• Watch the group corpus update in real time.
• Excess-outflow blocking means you never disburse more than you hold.

LEND AND REPAY
• Members request loans; the group approves by vote.
• Disburse, schedule repayments and track repayment health.
• Set loan ceilings and per-member limits.

SHARE OUT
• Calculate and distribute the end-of-cycle share-out — pro-rata, rotation payout or auction.

STAY TRANSPARENT
• Every member gets a personal dashboard of their savings, loans and requests.
• Notifications for meetings, approvals and payouts.
• Available in English, Swahili, French and Hindi.

MifosSave is open-source community banking from the Mifos Initiative — keeping every shilling accounted for, online or off.
